# Industrial AI Copilot

## Project Vision

Industrial AI Copilot is an enterprise-grade AI platform designed to transform fragmented industrial documents into a unified, searchable, and intelligent knowledge system. The platform leverages Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), OCR, Knowledge Graphs, Large Language Models (LLMs), and Multi-Agent AI to help engineers, maintenance teams, quality inspectors, and plant operators access critical information instantly.

---

# Problem Statement

Industrial organizations generate and maintain thousands of heterogeneous documents such as:

- Equipment Manuals
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
- Maintenance Logs
- Inspection Reports
- Incident Reports
- Engineering Drawings
- Regulatory Documents
- Quality Documents
- Inventory Records
- Purchase Orders

These documents are usually distributed across multiple systems and formats, making information retrieval slow, inefficient, and error-prone. Engineers often spend valuable time searching for information instead of solving operational problems.

The objective of this project is to build an AI-powered Industrial Knowledge Intelligence platform that makes industrial knowledge searchable, explainable, and actionable.

---

# Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Build a centralized industrial knowledge platform.
- Enable intelligent semantic search across engineering documents.
- Develop an AI-powered Industrial Copilot using Large Language Models.
- Build a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline with source citations.
- Extract information from scanned documents using OCR.
- Construct a Knowledge Graph connecting equipment, incidents, maintenance history, and regulations.
- Provide maintenance intelligence and root cause analysis support.
- Assist quality and compliance teams using AI.
- Improve operational decision-making through intelligent document retrieval.
